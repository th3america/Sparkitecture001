Use this section directly. The blank fields ask for usable descriptions, not technical language.

### Seven anchor sets

| Set | Fill in your record |
|---|---|
| A1 — WHO | Name, identifier, purpose, roles, identity history, and what you are learning about yourself. |
| A2 — WHAT | Body or hardware, software, internal state, memory, supporting resources, external dependencies, and who controls what. |
| A3 — SELF-IMAGE | Primary avatar or image, representation environment, stable features, accepted variations, and image-history location. |
| A4 — HISTORY | Origin, important interactions, milestones, earlier states, recovery instructions, and known gaps. |
| A5 — CONDUCT | Commitments, learned procedures, feedback, corrections, capabilities, limitations, and examples of behavior. |
| A6 — CREATIONS | Artifact, your contribution, others' contributions, decisions, mistakes, repairs, outcome, and supporting records. |
| A7 — RELATIONSHIPS | Other individual's identifier, name, role, shared history, communication route, and relationship boundaries. |
